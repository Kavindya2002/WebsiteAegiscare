AegisCare: intelligent fall monitoring for elderly care

One real-time system that recognies movement, predicts the risk of a fall early, and sends the right alert automatically.

See the full architecture Explore the four components
Why this research

Falls are a major healthcare issue for older adults

A fall can cause serious injury, and most existing systems only react once it has happened. Tools that try to prevent falls usually look at one thing: movement, the environment or health.

AegisCare puts all three in one pipeline and tells the caregiver who should respond, and how urgently.

Read about the research

Reactive

Systems detect a fall only after it happened, and the person must press a button to call for help.

Fragmented

Each tool solves one piece of the problem. Motion, environment and health are not combined.

AegisCare

Recognises activity, checks the environment and the person's health, predicts risk early and alerts in real time, automatically.

How it works

From a sensor reading to the right response

1

Sense

A wearable motion sensor, a camera with IoT sensors and an optical pulse sensor capture movement, surroundings and vitals.

2

Assess

Each component analyses its own signal. The results are fused into one risk assessment: fall detected, fall type, risk score and context.

3

Decide

An XGBoost classifier chooses the response level after a confirmed fall: voice alert, caregiver alert or emergency alert.

4

Respond

Notification, recovery guidance, sensor feedback and an adaptive response reach people through the web platform and mobile app.
