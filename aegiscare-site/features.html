Features

Four components, one intelligent system

Each component answers a different question, and together they cover the whole path from a person moving to the right person responding. Select a component for the details, the research gap and the system diagram.

01

Context-Aware Activity Recognition

A wearable IoT system that detects falls in elderly people in real time and explains every decision.

Member 1M T K Chathuri
02

Environmental Risk Mapping

Detect environmental risk (slippery floors, poor lighting, obstacles) through vision and IoT sensor fusion.

Member 2D H S Diwyangi
03

PPG-Based Biometric Fall Risk Prediction

Estimate heart rate, blood pressure and fall-risk status from optical vitals sensing.

Member 3W A H Divyanjalie
04

Intelligent Alert Classification

Turn every risk signal into the right intervention: voice warning, caregiver alert or emergency escalation.

Member 4E D K M Kulathunga
How they connect

Three sources of evidence feed one decision

Components 1, 2 and 3 sense

Movement, environment and vitals are each analysed by their own pipeline.

The assessment is fused

Results become one fused risk assessment: fall detected, fall type, risk score and context.

Component 4 decides

An XGBoost classifier picks the response level, so the right person is told at the right urgency.
